# 0002: Valuation Matrices, Auto-Dossier, and Historical Vernacular Tone

We adopt a 100-point normalized integer valuation framework, automated clue notebooks, and a classical court-drama tone (*Ming-Qing gongan* style) for *The Art of Scales*:

1. **Normalized 100-Point Valuation Matrices**: Each agent's preferences across a case sum to 100, allowing players to calculate and compare utility and envy rapidly without mental friction. Act II's matrix is strictly calibrated so feudal inheritance allocations fulfill EF1 while dramatically violating EFX.
2. **Automated Investigation Dossier**: Clicking characters and evidence in 3D Point & Click mode advances character dialogue and automatically logs exact subjective valuations into an on-screen dossier, freeing the player to focus on the ethical/mathematical arbitration puzzle rather than arithmetic note-taking.
3. **Hybrid Power/Silver Economics in Act III**: State power tokens (discrete, 100 pts per faction) combined with a continuous 100-point treasury silver slider demonstrate the transition from indivisible deadlocks (zero-product crash) to fractional convex optima (Fractional NSW).
4. **Historical Vernacular Tone**: Script and dialogue follow classical court drama cadence to evoke solemn ethical weight and institutional consequence.
