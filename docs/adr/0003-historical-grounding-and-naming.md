# 0003: Titling as 《定分止爭錄》 and Canonical Historical Citations

To elevate educational authority and narrative immersion beyond generic interactive games, the project is officially titled **《定分止爭錄》（The Art of Settled Shares）**, derived from *The Book of Lord Shang* (《商君書·定分》: "名分定，則私利行不苟；定分止爭，天下自治").

All three scenario acts are strictly anchored in authentic historical jurisprudence and court archives:
1. **Act I (Song Guild Dispute)**: Anchored in *The Clear and Bright Collection of Court Decisions* (《名公書判清明集》) and Song guild ordinances of Bianjing (Kaifeng).
2. **Act II (Ming Yamen Inheritance)**: Anchored in Ming archival household division indentures (*Huizhou Division Deeds* / 徽州分產鬮書), Hai Rui's prefectural verdicts (《海忠介公判詞》), and *The Great Ming Code* (《大明律》).
3. **Act III (Imperial Palace Council)**: Anchored in the actual historical triumvirate of the 4th year of the Wanli Emperor (1576 CE), featuring Emperor Shenzong, Grand Secretary Zhang Juzheng, Frontier Commander Qi Jiguang, and Chief Eunuch Feng Bao (*History of Ming* / 《明史·神宗本紀》, 《明史·張居正傳》, 《明史·戚繼光傳》).
