## 第一幕

### CS-01｜到任（對話場）

**意義**：新官到任的第一個畫面，交代程衡、周先生與那桿戥子；接著由周先生把第一個案子交到玩家手上。

**順序**：S1（6 秒，推近）→ S2（5 秒，定）→ 介面對話：周先生的整段台詞（劇本 CS-01）。

**立繪**：`zhou-calm` 單人置中，面向玩家。

**S1｜二堂**（從 `plate-act1` 改出）

畫面：金華府衙二堂，白天。程衡站在公案後方，正把戥子輕輕放到案上，低頭看著它。周先生從畫面右側的門口進來，懷裡抱著一疊用麻繩捆起的卷宗，看著程衡。兩人都是四分之三側面。

推鏡：推近，焦點在程衡與案上的戥子之間。

### CS-02｜鐵匠鋪的煙（靜圖）

**意義**：判決之後的一個畫面。哥哥回到田裡，弟弟的鋪子開張了。

**S1｜田埂**（8 秒，右移）

畫面：陰天的水田。陳守業站在水裡，褲管捲到膝上，手裡握著一把秧苗，剛直起腰，回頭望向遠處。遠方小城的西邊，一縷細細的灰煙從一間屋頂升起。他在畫面左三分之一，煙在右上。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-1]
A flooded rice paddy outside a small walled town in Zhejiang in spring. Standing knee-deep in the water on the left third of the frame: [chen-shouye], trousers rolled to the knee, a bundle of rice seedlings in one hand. He has just straightened his back and turns his head to look toward the far right, where a thin line of grey smoke rises from a single chimney at the western edge of the town. Seen from behind and to the side, his face in partial profile.
Composition: wide shot, 35mm lens, camera low near the water; rows of freshly planted seedlings lead the eye toward the town.
[AVOID]
```

**審圖重點**：赤腳；農人粗布衣，不是官服；煙只有一縷，不是火災。

### CS-03｜一封信（靜圖）

**意義**：伏筆。這封信讓戚繼光記住了程衡。

**S1｜油燈下**（7 秒，推近）

畫面：夜，陳從征坐在矮桌前，左手壓著紙，右手執筆在寫。油燈在畫面一側。紙上的字看不清。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-1]
Night, a small plain room in a village house lit only by one oil lamp. [chen-congzheng] sits at a low wooden table writing a letter: his left hand presses the sheet flat, his right hand holds a brush. His eyes are on the paper. Three-quarter view from his left side; the lamp at the edge of the frame lights his face and hands warmly.
The letter paper is in soft focus in the foreground. [PAPER]
Composition: medium close-up, 50mm lens, camera at table height.
[AVOID]
```

疊字：信的內容以字幕呈現（劇本 CS-03）。

**S2｜長城下的雪**（6 秒，左移）

畫面：黃昏，雪地。一名騎馬的信使沿著長城腳下的小路由右向左走，人和馬都很小。城牆沿山脊延伸。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
Dusk in deep winter. A lone courier on horseback, small in the frame, travels from right to left along a snowy track at the foot of the Ming Great Wall, which climbs along a mountain ridge behind him. Hoof prints in the snow, breath steaming from the horse. The rider wears a plain padded Ming dynasty traveling robe and a wide-brimmed rain hat, a cloth-wrapped satchel across his back.
Composition: extreme wide shot, 35mm lens, the wall occupying the upper half of the frame.
[AVOID]
```

字幕：「五年後 · 萬曆八年冬」。考成簿那一行字用介面的紙頁呈現，不需要圖。

**審圖重點**：城牆是明代磚砌城牆與敵臺；信使不戴清式帽子。
