## 第三幕

### CS-07｜一道命令的四天（混合）

**順序**：介面三格示意圖（擬稿 → 批准 → 檢查，周先生旁白）→ S1（第一天，6 秒）→ S2（第二天，6 秒）→ S3（第三天夜，6 秒）→ **V1**（第三天清晨，8 秒）→ S4（第四天，6 秒）。

S1、S2 用同一種構圖：俯拍書案上的一張紙和一隻手。兩張並排看，玩家會自己發現差別——一張是黑墨，一張是朱筆。

**S1｜擬稿**（定）

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
Top-down close-up of a dark wooden desk in the Grand Secretariat: a sheet of draft paper with six short columns of black brushwork, and an elderly official's hand in a scarlet sleeve laying a brush down beside it. [PAPER]
Composition: overhead shot, 50mm lens.
[AVOID]
```

疊字：「四件歸文官，兩件歸邊軍」＋字幕「宮裡一件也沒有。」

**S2｜批紅**（定）

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
Top-down close-up of the same kind of draft paper on a lacquered red desk, candlelit. A smooth, soft hand in a bright red silk sleeve, a string of dark prayer beads around the wrist, holds a brush loaded with vermilion ink and is striking through columns of black writing with bold red strokes. [PAPER]
Composition: overhead shot, 50mm lens.
[AVOID]
```

疊字：「四件歸宮裡，兩件留給文官」＋字幕「這回換成邊軍一件也沒有。」

**S3｜值房的夜**（推近）— 同時是 V1 起始畫面的主圖

畫面：六科值房，夜。程衡獨自坐在書案前，算盤在手邊，戥子壓在一張紙上。油燈。他看著那張紙。窗外是雪。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
[TONE-NIGHT]
A small, plain government office room at night in winter, snow visible through a lattice window. Seated alone at a desk: [CHENG-34]. In front of him lie an abacus and a sheet of paper held flat by [SCALE]. He looks down at the paper, very still, his fingers resting on the abacus. One oil lamp on the desk. Three-quarter view, facing frame-left. [PAPER]
Composition: medium shot, 50mm lens, camera at seated eye level, the window soft behind him.
[AVOID]
```

**V1｜「這道命令，我不能發。」**（8 秒，影片）

起始畫面：把 S3 改成清晨——「Same room, same man, same desk. Change the time to first light of dawn: the oil lamp has burned out, pale cold blue light comes through the window, he now looks up from the paper toward the window.」

運鏡：推近，85mm。

```text
Slow push-in from medium shot to close-up on his face.
[LIFE]
Action: he sits looking toward the window at frame-left, tired after a sleepless night. He breathes out a faint mist, then speaks quietly to himself, as a decision rather than a complaint. After the last word, his right hand lifts the steelyard scale off the paper.
Sound: early-morning silence, a distant crow, faint wind. He says in Mandarin Chinese, slowly and quietly: "這道命令，我不能發。" No music.
```

前面兩句獨白（「照首輔的稿……」「有一家是零……」）在 S3 上以旁白字幕呈現。

**S4｜召見**（拉遠）

畫面：清晨，六科廊外積雪。一名宦官提著燈籠站在值房門口，正對門內躬身傳話；門內只看得見一角青色衣袍。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
Early morning outside a row of plain palace office buildings after snowfall. A young palace eunuch in a dark robe and a black rounded cap without wings stands at an open office door holding an unlit paper lantern, bowing slightly as he speaks to someone inside. Through the doorway only the edge of a deep indigo-blue sleeve is visible. Footprints in fresh snow lead to the door.
Composition: wide shot, 35mm lens, side-on to the door.
[AVOID]
```

字幕：「滿朝的人都在等他被抓進大牢。」接「明日文華殿議事，叫那個退件的程衡也來。」（皇帝的話，以字幕呈現）

**審圖重點**：S3、V1 的程衡有短鬚；S1 與 S2 的紙張與構圖相同；S2 的手是馮保的手（無鬚之人柔軟的手、佛珠）。

**立繪**：兩人對話版式，張居正在左、程衡在右。`zhang-juzheng-aggrieved`（第一句）→ `cheng-heng-act3-calm` → `zhang-juzheng-pleased`（最後一句）。

畫面：雪剛停，文華門外的石階與朱牆。張居正停在門前，半轉身；程衡在他身後幾步，站定，雙手攏在袖中。兩人都是側面。機位在兩人連線的一側。

```text
[STILL]
[TONE-3]
Outside a vermilion palace gate in the Forbidden City just after snow has stopped, pale overcast light, fresh snow on the steps and the marble balustrades. [zhang-juzheng] has stopped on the steps and turns halfway back. A few paces behind him stands [CHENG-34], hands folded in his sleeves, waiting. Both seen in profile, facing each other across the distance. Breath mist in the cold.
Composition: wide shot, 35mm lens, camera at the side of the line between them; the red gate fills the background, no plaque visible.
```
